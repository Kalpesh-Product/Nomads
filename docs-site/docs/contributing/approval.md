sidebar_position: 3
---

The WONO team reviews your request and approves the contribution types you can create. Submitting the form does not give immediate access to the contributor tools.

| Option chosen | Roles requested |
| --- | --- |
| Nomad Blog & News Writer | Blogger and News Writer |
| Content Creator | Place Writer and Event Writer |

- You can request additional contribution types later. Your existing approved types remain available.
- Once you have approval, open Contributions from the sidebar.
- Your first approval email includes a link to Contributions.
- Only the contribution types approved for your account are available.
