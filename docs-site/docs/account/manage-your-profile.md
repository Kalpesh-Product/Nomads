Your account has four tabs: Profile, Change Password, Favorites and Reviews. Sign in, then open your account from the sidebar and choose the tab you need.

## Profile tab (?tab=profile)

Personal information with an Edit button. Fields: Full Name, Email, Current Country Of Residence, Mobile (with country code), Salary and Designation. After Edit the fields become editable and the button becomes Submit.

## Change Password tab (?tab=password)

Current Password, New Password and Confirm Password, with a list of password requirements below the fields. Submitting changes the password and signs the user out so they log in again.

## Favorites tab (?tab=favorites)

The Favorites tab gathers the destinations, businesses, blogs, news, places and events you have saved with a heart. Open an item to return to it, or use its remove control to take it out of Favorites.

## Reviews tab (?tab=reviews)

The Reviews tab gathers the reviews you have written for listings, events, places and restaurants. Find the review you want to change, then choose Edit or Delete.
