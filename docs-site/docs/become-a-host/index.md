If you operate a coworking space, hostel, cafe or other business, choose **Become a Host** on WONO to open the separate [Host product](https://hostpanel.wono.co/).

1. Compare the available plans and their monthly or annual prices.
2. Browse [Themes](./themes.md) to preview a website design or [Modules](./modules.md) to see the available tools, and read [Leads](./leads.md) for the marketing tools that bring enquiries in.
3. Choose **Get Started**, complete the business profile and follow the verification instructions sent to your email.

![Host landing page with pricing and plan cards](/img/guide/host-pricing.jpg)

![Host sign-up form for business details](/img/guide/host-sign-up.jpg)

## In this section

- [Modules](./modules.md) — the tools included with the platform.
- [Leads](./leads.md) — the marketing tools that generate organic leads.
- [Themes](./themes.md) — the website themes available to every host.

For help managing listings, enquiries or your business website after signup, use the [Host Panel documentation](https://hostpanel.wono.co/docs/).
