Open **Themes** to start with a website that already fits your business instead of building one from scratch.

[Open the Themes page](http://host.localhost:5173/themes)

![Host Themes page with the featured theme on a laptop and phone mockup](/img/guide/host-themes.webp)

## Featured theme

The top of the page previews one theme on a desktop and a phone mockup, with its business type alongside it. **Start building** opens the sign-up form, and **Explore themes** scrolls to the library. The page notes that every theme is responsive and comes with a live preview.

## Theme library

**Pick the experience that fits your business** shows every available theme as a card with its business type, name, colour palette and a short description. Each card offers:

- **View theme** to open the theme detail page with its inclusions and a **Preview** button.
- **Preview** to open the live website in a new tab.
- **Desktop** and **Mobile** badges, so you can check the layout on both before choosing.

Use the filter pills above the grid to narrow the themes to your business type, for example cafes, stays, workspaces or meeting rooms. The number of themes shown is displayed next to the heading. Themes that are not available yet appear as coming soon instead of in the grid.

For example, select **Cafe** to compare café layouts. Look at the card descriptions for features your customers will use, such as browsing a menu or reserving a table. Select **All** to return to the full library.

![Theme library filtered to Cafe, with theme cards and their View theme and Preview actions](/img/guide/host-theme-library.webp)

## Theme detail page

Choose **View theme** on a card to open its detail page. The breadcrumb at the top shows the business type and theme name. Under **Inclusions**, you can review the no-code website, booking engine, payment gateway, leads management, AI SEO and notifications. The desktop and phone mockups alongside the list show how the home page adapts to each screen.

![Host theme detail with inclusions and a Preview button](/img/guide/host-theme-detail.jpg)

Select **Preview** to try the website or **Get Started** to open host sign-up. Farther down, **The theme store promise** describes trial, updates and licensing. When other themes are available for the same business type, **Few more suggestions for you** lets you open another theme's detail page. Use the back arrow to return to the previous page or select **Themes** in the breadcrumb to go back to the full library.

![Suggested café themes underneath the inclusions on the Savor detail page](/img/guide/host-theme-suggestions.webp)

## Live theme preview

Choose **Preview** from the library card or the detail page to open the website in a new tab. Start on the home page, then use the site's navigation to explore the pages your customers would see. Most previews use sample content. Some themes, such as Biznest, open an existing live website.

![Savor preview home page with its navigation and reservation button](/img/guide/host-theme-preview.webp)

### Menu page

From Savor's home page, select **See the menu**. The **Our menu** page lets customers search for a dish, filter by categories such as **Coffee** or **Breakfast**, and sort by price or name. Item cards show photos, descriptions and prices.

![Savor menu page with its search field, dietary filters and category tabs](/img/guide/host-theme-preview-menu.webp)

### Contact and reservations

Open **Contact** to see the café's address, phone number, email and opening hours alongside the reservation form. The form asks for contact details, party size, a date and a time, with optional seating preferences and special requests.

![Savor contact page with the reservation form beside the café's contact details and hours](/img/guide/host-theme-preview-contact.webp)

Each theme has its own navigation. Stay and workspace themes may have pages for rooms or spaces. Check how visitors browse what you offer and reach a booking or enquiry form, then close the preview tab to return to the theme you were comparing.

## After choosing a theme

Choose **Start building** and complete the business profile to publish the site with your own brand, images and colours.

You can reach the same sign-up form through **Get Started** on the theme detail page. Fill in your business and contact details, then follow the verification instructions sent to your email. See the [Become a Host overview](./index.md) for the full sign-up flow.

![Host sign-up form for business details](/img/guide/host-sign-up.webp)
