Open **Leads** to see how the platform brings enquiries to your business. It describes the marketing tools that come with the Host product and the leads they produce.

[Open the Leads page](http://host.localhost:5173/leads)

![Host Leads page with the headline and social media banner](/img/guide/host-leads.webp)

## What the page shows

- A headline: generate continuous structured leads for your business with the support of the automated platform, plus **Get Started** to open the sign-up form.
- A banner of social media and content icons, followed by the promise of **organic leads with no investments**.
- A checklist of the channels used to bring the leads in: Google My Business, Google Maps, Google Reviews, Google SEO, Website, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Free Integrations, Cross Promotions, Database, WhatsApp.

## Automated Google lead sheet

Leads generated from the platform are collected in an automated Google sheet, so none of them are missed. The page shows an example of the sheet.

## Marketing tools

**Run & scale your business** lists the tools behind the leads:

- **SEO tools** — optimize the site for search and grow organic traffic.
- **Analytics** — reports with insights about the site and marketing activity.
- **Logo Maker** — an AI logo generator for the brand.
- **Google Ads with WoNo** — launch a Google Ads campaign and appear higher in search results.
- **Email marketing** — create and send campaigns, promotions and updates.
- **Google Business Profile** — manage the business on Google Maps, Search and more.
- **Facebook & Instagram Ads** — targeted campaigns to generate leads and traffic.
- **Marketing integrations** — connect Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel and CAPI to see traffic and visitor behaviour.
- **Social media marketing** — design, schedule and share content across platforms.

The page closes with examples of how Analytics helps get better ROI, a complete view of customers and the finer points of visitor behaviour.
