title: Become a Host
sidebar_position: 8
---

If you operate a coworking space, hostel, cafe or other business, choose **Become a Host** on WONO to open the separate [Host product](https://hostpanel.wono.co/).

1. Compare the available plans and their monthly or annual prices.
2. Browse **Themes** to preview a website design or **Modules** to see the available tools.
3. Choose **Get Started**, complete the business profile and follow the verification instructions sent to your email.

For help managing listings, enquiries or your business website after signup, use the [Host Panel documentation](https://hostpanel.wono.co/docs/).
