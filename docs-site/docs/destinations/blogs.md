Choose Blogs for the selected destination to see local guides and stories. Each card shows an image, title, author or source, and date. In Bali, examples include A Guide for Digital Nomads in Bali and The Best Cafes To Work From In Canggu, Bali. Open a card to read the full article.

![Published Bali blog cards with titles and dates](/img/guide/bali-blog-cards.jpg)

![A Guide for Digital Nomads in Bali article](/img/guide/bali-nomad-blog.jpg)

## Comments

Scroll below the article to the Comments section. The heading shows the current count, such as Comments (0) on A Guide for Digital Nomads in Bali, with a note that no comments have been posted yet and an invitation to be the first to share your thoughts.

Select the comment field to open the comment dialog. The dialog shows the article title in its header with a close button, your profile initials and name, five yellow stars, and a text box with the prompt Share your thoughts on this blog.

![Comment dialog on a Bali blog article with a name, star rating and comment box](/img/guide/bali-blog-comments.webp)

Choose a rating from one to five stars, type your comment in the box, then select Submit Comment to post it to the article. Use the close button in the corner to cancel without posting.
