Click the Events icon below the search bar to filter the listings to events. The icon becomes underlined, the heading changes to Popular Annual Events in Bali and the page shows only event cards such as Bali Arts Festival in Denpasar, Bali Kite Festival across Bali, Bali Spirit Festival in Gianyar Regency, Lovina Festival in Buleleng Regency and Makepung Buffalo Racing in Jembrana, each with its listed dates or season. Scroll down for more events, or choose All Listing to see every type of business again.

![Bali listings filtered to annual events with the Events icon selected](/img/guide/listings-filter-events.webp)

Event pages show what an event is, where it takes place and its listed dates or season. The Bali Arts Festival example shows Denpasar and June-July. Read the description, save or share the event, and check the organiser's current schedule before planning your visit.

![Bali Arts Festival page with its image, location and season](/img/guide/bali-arts-festival.jpg)
