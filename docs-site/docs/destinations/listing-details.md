Open a business card to see its photos, description, facilities, reviews and location. The example is Karya Co-working Bali in Canggu. Use this page to decide whether the space suits your needs and request a quote.

![Karya Co-working Bali with its photos and business name](/img/guide/listing-detail.png)

- Browse the main photo and thumbnails. Select the photos or more button to open the full gallery.
- Read About to understand the business. The rating and review count help you compare other visitors' experiences.
- To request a quote, enter your full name, number of people, mobile number, email and start and end dates in Enquire & Receive Quote. Select Get Quote and check the confirmation.
- Scroll to the inclusions to check facilities such as Wi-Fi, meeting rooms and printing. Use the location section to find the business and open directions.
- Read the reviews for other visitors' experiences. Choose Write a Review to share your own experience after signing in.
- Use the heart icon to save a business to Favorites. Use Share to copy a link for someone else.

## Enquire & Receive Quote

The Enquire & Receive Quote form sits beside the About section, so you can ask about a stay while you read the description.

![Enquire & Receive Quote form beside the About section](/img/guide/listing-detail-enquiry-form.webp)

1. Enter your Full Name.
2. Set No. Of People with the minus and plus buttons.
3. Enter your Mobile Number and pick the country flag for your dialling code.
4. Enter the Email address where you want to receive the quote.
5. Choose a Start Date and End Date from the calendar icons.
6. Select Get Quote to send the enquiry.

The business receives your dates and party size, and replies with a quote. Enter your details carefully, because the host uses the mobile number and email to contact you.

## Reviews

The reviews section shows the overall score at the top, followed by a Guest Favorite badge when the business is one of the most loved places on WONO, based on ratings, reviews and reliability. Karya Co-working Bali shows 4.9 out of 5 from 100 reviews.

![Reviews section with the overall score and review cards](/img/guide/listing-detail-reviews.webp)

Each review card shows the reviewer's initials and name, a star rating, the start of their review and a Show more link to read the rest. Select View more to see all reviews for the business. Read a few reviews before you book to compare the space, internet and working atmosphere with what you need.

## Write a review

Select Write a Review under the review cards to share your own experience. You need to be signed in first; signed-out visitors are asked to log in.

![Write a review dialog with a star rating and comment box](/img/guide/listing-detail-write-review.webp)

The dialog shows the business name and your name with your profile initials. Select the stars to rate the business from 1 to 5, write about your stay in the text box, then select Submit Review. Your review then appears with the others on the business page.

## Where you'll be

The Where You'll Be section at the bottom of the page locates the business on a map, so you can check the area before you request a quote.

![Map showing the business marker, address and directions button](/img/guide/listing-detail-map.webp)

Select the marker to see the business name, full address and its rating and review count. Select Open in Maps to open the location in your maps app and get directions from where you are. Use the zoom and fullscreen buttons to look at nearby streets, beaches and places to eat.
