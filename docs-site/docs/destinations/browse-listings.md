Open a destination and select **Map view** beside the WONO logo to compare listings by location. On desktop, business cards appear next to the map. In the example below, **All Listing** is selected, so the cards include co-living, coworking and meeting spaces in Bali. The search bar shows the path **Asia → Indonesia → Bali**.

![Bali Map View with All Listing selected, business cards on the left and map pins and numbered groups on the right](/img/guide/destination-listings-map.png)

- Drag the map to explore nearby areas. Numbered circles group nearby listings. Zoom in to see individual pins.
- On desktop, hover over a pin to preview the business. Select a pin or open a business card to see the listing details.
- Choose **Map** for the street map, **Satellite** for aerial imagery, or the full-screen control to expand the map.
- Select **List view** beside the WONO logo to return to [Destination Listings - List View](./destination-hub.md).

## Filter the listings

Select a category below the search bar to narrow the business cards and map to that type. In the example below, **Co-Working** is underlined, the search bar includes a **Co-Working** chip, and the heading changes to **Popular Co-Working Spaces in Bali**. The cards show each space’s photo, area and rating, such as Karya Co-working Bali in Canggu. Select **All Listing** to show every category again.

![Bali Map View filtered to Co-Working, with the category chip, coworking cards and map visible](/img/guide/destination-listings-map-co-working.png)
