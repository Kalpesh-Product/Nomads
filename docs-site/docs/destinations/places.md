Click the Places icon below the search bar to filter the listings to places to visit. The icon becomes underlined, the heading changes to Popular Places to visit in Bali and the page shows only place cards such as Banyumala Waterfall, Bayan Ancient Tree, Jatiluwih Rice Terraces, Diamond Beach and Goa Rang Reng Waterfall, each with its area and star rating. Scroll down for more places, or choose All Listing to see every type of business again.

![Bali listings filtered to places to visit with the Places icon selected](/img/guide/listings-filter-places.webp)

Place pages help you explore local attractions. The Banyumala Waterfall example shows the name, address, image, type of attraction and rating. Read the description, use Get Direction for the location, or save and share the place. Sign in to write a review.

![Banyumala Waterfall page with its photo, address and rating](/img/guide/banyumala-waterfall.jpg)

## Reviews

Select Write a Review under the description to share your own experience at the place. You need to be signed in first.

![Write a review dialog on the place page with a star rating and comment box](/img/guide/place-write-review.webp)

The dialog shows the place name and your name with your profile initials. Select the stars to rate the place from 1 to 5, write about your visit in the text box, then select Submit Review. Your review then appears with the others on the place page.

## Where you'll be

The Where You'll Be section at the bottom of the place page locates the attraction on a map, so you can check the area and plan your route before you visit.

![Map showing the Banyumala Waterfall marker, its address and the Open in Maps button](/img/guide/place-map-view.webp)

Select the marker to see the place name, full address and its rating and review count. Banyumala Waterfall shows 4.8 stars from 5,696 reviews. Select Open in Maps to open the location in your maps app and get directions from where you are. Use the zoom and fullscreen buttons to look at nearby roads, villages and other places to visit.
