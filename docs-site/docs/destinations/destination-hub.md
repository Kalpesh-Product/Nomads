The Destinations Listings page brings local options together in one place. In Bali, it includes coworking spaces such as Karya Co-working Bali, hostels such as Rodin Bali Hostel, and cafes such as BRIGHT coffee and brunch. Each business card shows a photo, name, area and rating. Open a card to see the details.

![Bali Destinations Listings page with business photos, names and ratings](/img/guide/destination-hub.png)

- Choose a category such as Co-Working, Co-Living, Hostels, Meetings or Cafes to see that type of business. Use View more to browse the full category.
- Value Adds provides shortcuts to Visa Support, Activation Support, Company Setup and Consultation. An option marked Coming soon is not yet available.
- Continue down the page for annual events, places to visit, news and blogs. For example, Bali includes the Bali Arts Festival, Banyumala Waterfall and guides for digital nomads.
- Choose Map view or Show map to compare locations. Zoom into the map and select a marker or a matching business card to explore further.

## Filter the listings

Click one of the icons below the search bar to filter the listings by type. The selected icon is underlined, the results heading changes to that type and the page shows only matching businesses. For example, choosing Co-Working changes the heading to Popular Co-Working Spaces in Bali and lists coworking spaces such as Nebula Entrepreneur Coworking Space in Tububeneng, AT 06 in Canggu, PUCO Rooftop Coworking in Tububeneng, BukitHub in Ungasan and HEDONIST CO-WORKING in Ungasan, each with its area and star rating. Choose All Listings to see every type of business again.
