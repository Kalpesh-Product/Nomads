The destination hub brings local options together on one page. In Bali, it includes coworking spaces such as Karya Co-working Bali, hostels such as Rodin Bali Hostel, and cafes such as BRIGHT coffee and brunch. Each business card shows a photo, name, area and rating. Open a card to see the details.

![Bali destination hub with business photos, names and ratings](/img/guide/destination-hub.png)

- Choose a category such as Co-Working, Co-Living, Hostels, Meetings or Cafes to see that type of business. Use View more to browse the full category.
- Value Adds provides shortcuts to Visa Support, Activation Support, Company Setup and Consultation. An option marked Coming soon is not yet available.
- Continue down the hub for annual events, places to visit, news and blogs. For example, Bali includes the Bali Arts Festival, Banyumala Waterfall and guides for digital nomads.
- Choose Map view or Show map to compare locations. Zoom into the map and select a marker or a matching business card to explore further.
