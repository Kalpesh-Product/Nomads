Choose News to browse stories for the destination. Cards show the title, image, source and date. Open a story to read its text and sections, then use the heart or Share button to save or share it. The example article is Digital Nomads in Bali Embrace eSIM for Seamless Connectivity.

![Bali news cards with published stories](/img/guide/bali-news-cards.jpg)

![News article about eSIM connectivity for digital nomads in Bali](/img/guide/bali-connectivity-news.jpg)

## Comments

Scroll below the story to the Comments section. The heading shows the current count, such as Comments (0) on Digital Nomads in Bali Embrace eSIM for Seamless Connectivity, with a note that no comments have been posted yet and an invitation to be the first to share your thoughts.

Select the comment field to open the comment dialog. The dialog shows the article title in its header with a close button, your profile initials and name, five yellow stars, and a text box with the prompt Share your thoughts on this news article.

![Comment dialog on a Bali news article with a name, star rating and comment box](/img/guide/bali-news-comments.webp)

Choose a rating from one to five stars, type your comment in the box, then select Submit Comment to post it to the story. Use the close button in the corner to cancel without posting.
