title: "Status pages"
sidebar_position: 7
---

Two static pages exist for outages. /site-is-down says the system is down and asks the visitor to try again later. /website-under-maintenance says the site is under maintenance and will be back shortly. Neither is linked from the menus; they are reached directly or by redirect.
