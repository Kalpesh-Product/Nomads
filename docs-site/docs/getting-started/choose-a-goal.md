On the WONO home page, choose the goal that best matches what you want from your next destination. Each goal opens a different search.

![Home page for a logged-out visitor](/img/guide/home.png)

## The six goal cards

| Card | Purpose | Login needed? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World Ranking | Best nomad destinations by the WONO world index, which combines 50+ global factors | No |
| Work From Anywhere | Suggestions for the best places to work remotely | Yes |
| Increase Your Savings | Destinations suited to saving more as a nomad | Yes |
| Advance Your Career | Destinations that suit career growth | Yes |
| Find Your Community | Like-minded people and communities as per preferences | Yes |
| Classic Search | Manual continent, country and location selection | No |

Each card shows a small label under it, either “No login required” or “Login required”. Choosing a gated card while logged out sends the visitor to the login page, with a message explaining why, and returns them to their goal after they sign in.
