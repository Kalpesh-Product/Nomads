The Nomad pages share a header, a collapsible sidebar, a back button and a footer. Use the sidebar to move between discovery, support and account features.

## Header

- WONO logo returns to the home page.
- Logged-out visitors see Login as Nomad and Become a Host. Logged-in users see Become a Host and an avatar for their account.

## Sidebar

The sidebar groups features into three sections. When collapsed it shows only the abbreviations below.

| Section | Short | Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WoNo Intelligence | WOI | World Ranking, Work From Anywhere, Increase Your Savings, Advance Your Career, Find Your Community, Classic Search |
| Value-Added Services | VAS | VISA Support, Activation Support, Company Setup, Consultation, Workation, Apply for Jobs |
| Profile | PRO | Account name, Favorites, Reviews, Change Password, Contributions (when approved), Become a Contributor, Become a Host, Sign Out |

## Guided tours

On your first visit to some pages, a short tour points out the main controls. Select Next to continue or close the tour to explore on your own. The browser remembers completed tours.
