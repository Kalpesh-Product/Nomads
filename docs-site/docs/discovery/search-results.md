Choose a region in Where Do You Want To Go? and a preference in Choose Your Goal! The results show ranked destinations for those choices. Use the dropdowns again whenever you want to compare a different region or goal.

![Loaded destination cards with rankings, favorites and comparison scores](/img/guide/ranked-results.png)

- Where Do You Want To Go? narrows by continent (Explore The World, Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania, South America).
- Choose Your Goal switches the lens for the current goal (see the individual goal pages).
- When a visa filter is available, use it to narrow the results by visa type for your passport.
- Destination cards show the city and country, ranking and comparison scores. In the example, the scores cover work infrastructure, internet, affordability and community. Use them to compare the destinations.
- The heart saves a destination to Favorites. Logged-out visitors are sent to log in first and return to the same results page.
- Clicking a card opens the Verticals page for that destination ([Destination hub](../destinations/destination-hub.md)).
- Scroll position and filters are remembered when the visitor goes back.
