Choose Increase Your Savings from the home page or sidebar. Select a region and a ranking goal, such as Maximum Savings, to compare destinations. Open a destination card to see places to work, stay and explore.

In results, the Increase Your Savings goal adds these lenses: Maximum Savings, Low Taxation, Purchasing Power, Financial Stability(Low Risk), Startup Setup Cost and Balanced Financial Lifestyle.
