sidebar_position: 6
---

Choose Find Your Community from the home page or sidebar. Select a region and a ranking goal, such as Social & Party Lifestyle, to compare destinations. Open a destination card to see places to work, stay and explore.

In results, the Find Your Community goal adds these lenses: Social & Party Lifestyle, Chill & Wellness, Adventure & Exploration, Nomad Community & Networking, Couple, Family and Female Friendly, Founder Nomads and Solo Nomads.
