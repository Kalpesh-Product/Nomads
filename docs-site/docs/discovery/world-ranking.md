title: "World Ranking"
sidebar_position: 2
---

Choose World Ranking from the home page or sidebar. Select a region and a ranking goal, such as Best for Nomads, to compare destinations. Open a destination card to see places to work, stay and explore.

Inside results, World Ranking offers these ranking lenses: Best for Nomads, Most Affordable, Safest Cities, Easy Visa / Long Stay, Strong Nomad Community, Healthcare Friendly, Startup / Business Opportunities, Clean Air / Environment and Best Work Infrastructure.
